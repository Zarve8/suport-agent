## Page 1

## Overview

Valetax offers a range of instant, simple, and secure funding methods intended
to cater to different customer requirements and preferences.

## How to deposit funds

### Step 1: Log in

Log in to your Valetax Member Area.

### Step 2: Select a payment channel

Click the **Deposit** button and select your preferred payment channel.

### Step 3: Enter account details and deposit amount

Enter your account details and the amount you want to deposit.

### Step 4: Start trading

You can start trading once the deposited balance has been updated.

## Deposit questions and answers

### What is the minimum deposit amount?

The minimum deposit starts from **8 USD**, depending on the account type.

### How can I deposit funds into my account?

Log in to your Member Area and select the preferred payment option and deposit
size.

### What currencies can I use to deposit funds?

You may deposit using any preferred currency into the Valetax USD account. The
deposit will follow that day's exchange rates.

### How long does it take for deposited funds to be credited to my balance?

Deposits are described as instant. The updated account balance should be
visible within **60 minutes**.

### When will my funds be available for trading?

You can begin trading immediately once your balance has been updated.

### Where can I view my deposit history?

Log in to your Member Area to view transactions related to deposits,
withdrawals, and transfers.
