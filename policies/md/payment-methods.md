intended to support quicker withdrawals while keeping fees minimal.

## Deposit questions and answers

### What is the minimum deposit amount?

The page states that the minimum deposit amount is **USD 1**, depending on the
account type.

### How can I deposit funds into my account?

Log in to the Member Area and select the preferred payment option and deposit
size.

### What currencies can I use to deposit funds?

You may deposit using any preferred currency into the Valetax USD account. The
transaction follows that day's exchange rates.

### How long does it take for deposited funds to be credited to my balance?

Deposits are described as instant. The updated account balance should be
visible within **60 minutes**.

### When will my funds be available for trading?

You can begin trading immediately once your balance has been updated.

### Where can I view my deposit and withdrawal history?

Log in to the Member Area to view transactions related to deposits, withdrawals,
and transfers.

## Withdrawal-related information on this page

The page also contains the following withdrawal information:

- A pending withdrawal request means the request is in a queue and being
  processed. The customer will be notified once it is approved.
- A rejected withdrawal may be related to an unverified account, an error in
  the entered data, or insufficient margin. The company says it will notify the
  customer by email of the rejection reason.
- If funds have not been received within **24 hours** after a withdrawal
  request, the customer should contact support.
